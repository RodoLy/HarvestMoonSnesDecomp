Harvest Moon (SNES) - native build, v0.1.0 beta
================================================

The game runs on its code rewritten in C, built on your own computer.
No ROM, no generated code and no ready-made program are included:
you need your own legal copy of Harvest Moon (USA).

WINDOWS 10/11 (x64 or ARM)
 1. Double-click build.bat. The first time it downloads a C compiler
    (llvm-mingw) and SDL3, about 190 MB, checked by their SHA-256.
    Then it builds the game in about a minute.
 2. Open HarvestMoonNative.exe.
 3. The first time, choose your ROM (Harvest Moon USA, .sfc or .smc).
    It is checked and remembered.

MAC (Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 11+)
 1. Double-click build_mac.command (if macOS says it is from an
    unidentified developer: right-click it > Open > Open). The first
    time it may ask to install Apple's command line tools, and it
    downloads CMake (50 MB) and SDL3's source. It builds in a few minutes.
 2. Double-click "Harvest Moon.command" and choose your ROM.

LINUX
    cmake -S native -B build/native && cmake --build build/native
    build/native/HarvestMoonNative /path/to/your/rom.sfc

Everything starts OFF: the game is the console's. Turn on what you like
in the launcher (Esc): Improvements (fast text, longer days, the
Harvest Moon 64-style pause menu, OPTIONS on the title screen, the
endless game, widescreen, cheats) and Options.

Keys: arrows, X = A, Z = B, S = X, A = Y, C = L, V = R, Enter = Start,
Right Shift = Select. Esc launcher, M pause menu, Tab fast forward,
F11 full screen, F1-F10 load a state, Shift+F1-F10 save one.
A gamepad works too. Everything can be changed in Controls.

This is a beta: please report problems in the repository's Issues.
https://github.com/RodriLoy/HarvestMoonSnesDecomp

Harvest Moon (SNES) - version nativa, v0.1.0 beta

El juego corre sobre su codigo reescrito en C, compilado en tu propia
computadora. No incluye la ROM, ni codigo generado, ni un programa ya
hecho: necesitas tu propia copia legal de Harvest Moon (USA).

WINDOWS 10/11 (x64 o ARM)
 1. Doble clic en build.bat (o compilar_nativo.bat). La primera vez
    descarga un compilador de C (llvm-mingw) y SDL3, unos 190 MB,
    revisados por su SHA-256. Luego compila el juego en un minuto.
 2. Abre HarvestMoonNative.exe.
 3. La primera vez elige tu ROM (Harvest Moon USA, .sfc o .smc). Se
    revisa y se recuerda.

MAC (Apple Silicon o Intel, macOS 11+)
 1. Doble clic en build_mac.command (si macOS dice que es de un
    desarrollador no identificado: clic derecho > Abrir > Abrir). La
    primera vez puede pedir instalar las herramientas de linea de
    comandos de Apple, y descarga CMake (50 MB) y el codigo de SDL3.
    Compila en unos minutos.
 2. Doble clic en "Harvest Moon.command" y elige tu ROM.

Todo viene APAGADO: el juego es el de la consola. Activa lo que quieras
en el launcher (Esc): Mejoras y Opciones. El idioma se cambia en
Options > Language.
